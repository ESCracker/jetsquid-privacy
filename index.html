鱿往直前（Jet Squid）隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 13 日

本政策说明「鱿往直前」（以下称“本游戏”）如何处理你的信息。本游戏由独立开发者开发。请在使用前阅读本政策。

一、我们收集的信息

1. 我们不收集的信息

本游戏不要求注册账号，不收集你的姓名、电话号码、邮箱、通讯录、照片、精确位置或麦克风/摄像头内容。开发者没有自己的服务器，也不会把你的个人信息上传到开发者处。

2. 保存在你手机上的数据

以下数据只保存在你的设备本地，不会上传：游戏进度（通关记录、最远距离、珍珠数量、角色等级、装备、成就、水族馆状态、竞速成绩）以及你的设置（音效、音乐、震动、操作方式、语言）。卸载本游戏会一并删除这些数据。

3. 第三方服务收集的信息

本游戏使用以下第三方服务，它们可能会收集信息：

  • Google AdMob（广告）：用于展示广告。可能收集广告标识符（Advertising ID）、设备型号与系统版本、粗略位置（基于 IP）、广告互动数据，用于投放和衡量广告效果。详见 Google 广告政策。
  • Google Play 结算服务（内购）：处理应用内购买。购买记录由 Google 处理，开发者不会接触到你的银行卡号或支付密码。详见 Google 隐私权政策。

二、广告与你的选择

本游戏包含横幅/插屏广告和激励视频广告（观看后可获得游戏内奖励，观看与否完全自愿）。

  • 在欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士，首次启动时会出现广告同意窗口。你可以随时在游戏内「设置 → 广告隐私设置」中修改选择。
  • 你可以在手机系统设置中重置或删除广告标识符，以限制个性化广告。路径通常为：设置 → Google → 广告。
  • 购买「去除广告」后，本游戏不再展示插屏广告，原本需要看广告的奖励可直接领取。

三、儿童隐私

本游戏面向一般受众，并非专门面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会主动收集儿童的个人信息。如果你认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请通过下方邮箱联系，我们会尽快删除。

四、数据安全与保留

游戏数据保存在你的设备上，由设备自身的安全机制保护。开发者不持有你的个人数据，因此也不会保留或共享这些数据。第三方服务的数据保留期限，请参见其各自的隐私政策。

五、你的权利

你可以随时删除本地游戏数据（卸载应用）、关闭个性化广告，或联系我们询问与你相关的数据处理情况。根据你所在地区的法律（如 GDPR、CCPA），你可能享有访问、更正、删除数据以及拒绝个性化广告的权利。

六、政策变更

本政策如有更新，我们会修改本页面顶部的“最后更新”日期。重大变更会在游戏内另行提示。

Jet Squid — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how Jet Squid (the “Game”) handles your information. The Game is made by an independent developer. Please read this policy before using the Game.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 What we do not collect

The Game requires no account or sign-in. We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data. The developer operates no server of their own and does not receive your personal information.

1.2 Data stored on your device

The following is stored locally on your device only and is never uploaded: game progress (worlds cleared, best distance, pearls, squid levels, gear, awards, aquarium state, race times) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, controls, language). Uninstalling the Game deletes this data.

1.3 Information collected by third parties

The Game uses the following third-party services, which may collect information:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): serves ads and may collect your advertising identifier, device model and OS version, coarse location (from IP address) and ad interaction data, in order to deliver and measure ads. See Google’s advertising policies.
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases): processes purchases. Transactions are handled by Google; the developer never receives your card number or payment credentials. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

2. Ads and Your Choices

The Game shows interstitial ads and optional rewarded video ads (watching one grants an in-game reward; watching is always voluntary).

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent dialog appears on first launch. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy in the Game.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings to limit personalized ads, usually under Settings → Google → Ads.
  • After purchasing “Remove ads”, no interstitial ads are shown and ad-gated rewards are granted instantly.

3. Children’s Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it promptly.

4. Data Security and Retention

Game data is stored on your device and protected by your device’s own security. The developer holds no personal data about you and therefore retains and shares none. For third-party retention periods, see their privacy policies.

5. Your Rights

You may delete local game data at any time by uninstalling the Game, opt out of personalized ads, or contact us with questions about your data. Depending on where you live (e.g. under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete data and to object to personalized advertising.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be announced in the Game.
